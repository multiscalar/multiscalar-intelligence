Science in the Open

Multiscalar Intelligence is a research company, and we publish what we find. We believe open science is how a field advances: proofs, benchmarks, code and results released where anyone can check them, including the results that did not go our way. Our contributions begin with the foundations the rest of our work depends on, mathematics, mechanism design and economics, and extend to the multi-agent systems built on top of them. Everything on this page stands on its own, independent of any product.

Discovered by the Multiscalar Fields System

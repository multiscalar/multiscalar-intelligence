We prove that the natural density *d<sub>k</sub>(p)*, of integers whose
*k*-th smallest prime divisor is *p*, is **not unimodal for every *k* ≥ 4**,
completing Erdős' classification. The proof was discovered by the
Multiscalar Fields System with limited human interaction.

The Economic Intelligence Index will compare AI models on how well they make economic decisions: negotiating, planning, pricing and allocating capital. It brings the benchmarks we run, and those we host from partners, into one comparable score across five dimensions.

## What the preview shows

The preview on our evals page uses example data. It shows the shape of the index, the dimensions it measures, and how individual benchmarks will feed into it.

## What comes next

We will publish the methodology alongside the first scored release, and open the underlying data to researchers and labs on request.
